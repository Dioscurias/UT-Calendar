Prefer a calendar file instead?
  1. Open calendar.google.com → Settings → Import & export.
  2. Under "Import", select the .ics file you downloaded and choose a calendar.
  3. Click Import — every class is added at once.
Give each class its own color

Neither method above carries per-class colors automatically. After adding your classes, click any occurrence of one, choose "All events", and pick a color — that recolors its whole recurring series at once. Repeat per class.
