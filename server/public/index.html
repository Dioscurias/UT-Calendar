UT Class Schedule → Google Calendar

Screenshot your class list, drop it below, verify the extracted classes, and download a calendar file for the whole semester.

1 Add a screenshot

Log in to utdirect.utexas.edu, open your class list / schedule page, and take a screenshot (Mac: ⌘⇧4 then drag, or ⌘⌃⇧4 to copy straight to your clipboard). Make sure your whole schedule is visible before capturing.

Click to choose a file, drag one here, or paste (⌘V) a screenshot from your clipboard

2 Verify / edit your classes

Check what got extracted — vision reading can occasionally misread a time or room. Click day letters to toggle.

Course Days Start End Location

3 Semester dates

Defaults are UT Austin's official Fall 2026 dates. Adjust if you're on a different semester/campus.

4 Generate

Import into Google Calendar

  1. Open calendar.google.com → Settings → Import & export.
  2. Under "Import", click Select file from your computer and choose the .ics file you just downloaded.
  3. Pick which calendar to add the classes to (consider creating a new one called "Fall 2026 Classes" first, so it's easy to remove later).
  4. Click Import.
